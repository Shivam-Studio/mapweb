# Starter map data attribution

## Editable country boundaries

The editable country-boundary geometry in the starter maps is based on the user-supplied amCharts world SVG. The original SVG includes the visible **Map by amCharts** credit. amCharts makes individual free SVG maps available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0), provided the attribution is retained.

- Source: https://www.amcharts.com/svg-maps/world/
- amCharts map download / licensing information: https://amcharts.com/svg-maps/download/
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Required credit: **Map by amCharts** — https://www.amcharts.com/svg-maps/
- Changes made here: SVG boundary data was recolored/restyled, cropped into regional map packs, and paired with separate illustrative generated relief textures. The source attribution is retained in the SVG where present and is also shown in Map Library cards and this notice.

## Physical relief artwork

The physical relief imagery shipped with the World Physical and continent starter maps is project-generated illustrative shaded-relief artwork. It is **not** a measured DEM, satellite image, or authoritative elevation dataset.

No third-party satellite imagery is included in this starter collection.
